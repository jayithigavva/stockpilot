# StockPilot Pricing & Feature Tiers

## Overview

StockPilot offers two tiers designed to help footwear brands of all sizes optimize their inventory decisions. The free tier provides essential tools to get started, while the premium tier unlocks the full power of AI-driven inventory optimization.

---

## 🆓 FREE TIER

**Target:** Small footwear brands, startups, or brands testing the platform

### Core Features (Always Free)

#### 1. **Basic Inventory Management**
- ✅ Add and manage up to **50 products**
- ✅ Track inventory levels manually
- ✅ Basic inventory dashboard (total units, total value)
- ✅ View inventory by style and size
- ✅ Basic inventory alerts (low stock warnings)

#### 2. **Product & Style Management**
- ✅ Create and manage footwear styles
- ✅ Add products with size, color, width attributes
- ✅ Link products to styles
- ✅ Basic product catalog

#### 3. **Sales History Tracking**
- ✅ Upload and view sales history
- ✅ Basic sales analytics (last 30 days)
- ✅ Manual data entry

#### 4. **Supplier Management**
- ✅ Add and manage suppliers
- ✅ Track supplier information
- ✅ Basic supplier notes

#### 5. **Limited AI Recommendations**
- ✅ **5 AI recommendations per month**
- ✅ Basic reorder suggestions (quantity only)
- ✅ Simple risk indicators (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH)
- ✅ No size-level breakdowns
- ✅ No capital allocation
- ✅ No advanced explanations

#### 6. **Basic Dashboard**
- ✅ Inventory overview (total units, styles count)
- ✅ Cash management (view available cash)
- ✅ Pending decisions count
- ✅ Basic metrics only

#### 7. **Settings & Account**
- ✅ User account management
- ✅ Basic brand settings
- ✅ Free plan expiration tracking

### Limitations

- ❌ **50 products maximum**
- ❌ **5 AI recommendations per month**
- ❌ **No size-level optimization** (style-level only)
- ❌ **No capital allocation** across multiple SKUs
- ❌ **No advanced risk analysis** (basic categories only)
- ❌ **No Monte Carlo simulations** (simplified forecasts)
- ❌ **No detailed explanations** of AI decisions
- ❌ **No historical decision tracking**
- ❌ **No export capabilities**
- ❌ **No API access**
- ❌ **No priority support**

## 🔧 Technical Implementation Notes

### Plan Type Storage

**Database Schema:**
```sql
-- brands table
plan_type TEXT DEFAULT 'free'  -- 'free' or 'premium'
free_plan_started_at TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT NOW()
available_cash DECIMAL(10, 2) DEFAULT 0
```

### Feature Gating Logic

**Backend (Python/FastAPI):**
```python
# Check plan limits
def check_product_limit(brand_id, db):
    if plan_type == 'free':
        product_count = db.query(Product).filter(...).count()
        if product_count >= 50:
            raise HTTPException(400, "Free plan limit: 50 products max")
    
def check_ai_recommendation_limit(brand_id, db):
    if plan_type == 'free':
        this_month = count_recommendations_this_month(brand_id)
        if this_month >= 5:
            raise HTTPException(400, "Free plan: 5 recommendations/month limit")
```

**Frontend (React/Next.js):**
```typescript
// Show upgrade prompts
{plan_type === 'free' && productCount >= 45 && (
  <UpgradePrompt message="You're approaching the 50 product limit" />
)}

{plan_type === 'free' && recommendationsThisMonth >= 4 && (
  <UpgradePrompt message="Only 1 recommendation left this month" />
)}
```

### AI Feature Gating

**Free Tier AI:**
- Use simplified forecasting (single-point estimate, not quantile)
- Skip Monte Carlo simulation
- Style-level recommendations only (no size breakdown)
- Basic risk categories (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH) without probability
- No capital allocation

**Premium Tier AI:**
- Full LightGBM quantile regression (P10, P50, P90)
- Monte Carlo simulation (5000+ paths)
- Size-level optimization with breakdowns
- Advanced risk estimation (stockout probability, days of cover)
- Capital allocation across SKUs
- Detailed explanations

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Free Tier

**Q: How long is the free trial?**
A: 30 days of full premium access, then limited to free tier features.

**Q: What happens after 30 days?**
A: You keep all your data, but AI recommendations are limited to 5/month, and product limit is 50.

**Q: Can I upgrade later?**
A: Yes! You can upgrade anytime. All your data is preserved.

**Q: Do I need a credit card for free tier?**
A: No credit card required for free tier.

**Q: What if I exceed 50 products?**
A: You'll need to upgrade to premium or remove some products.

### Premium Tier

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?**
A: Yes, cancel anytime. No long-term contracts.

**Q: Is there a setup fee?**
A: No setup fees. Just monthly subscription.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Credit cards, debit cards, UPI, bank transfers (India). International cards accepted.

**Q: Do you offer annual plans?**
A: Yes! Annual plans get 2 months free (save 16%).

**Q: Can I downgrade from premium to free?**
A: Yes, but you'll need to reduce products to 50 or less, and lose access to premium features.

**Q: What happens to my data if I cancel?**
A: Your data is preserved for 90 days. You can reactivate anytime within 90 days.

### AI Recommendations

**Q: Why are free tier recommendations limited?**
A: AI processing is computationally expensive. Free tier gives you a taste, premium unlocks full power.

**Q: What's the difference between free and premium AI?**
A: Free: basic quantity suggestions. Premium: size-level optimization, capital allocation, detailed explanations, risk analysis.

**Q: How accurate are the recommendations?**
A: Our AI uses advanced ML techniques (LightGBM, Monte Carlo simulation). Accuracy depends on data quality, but typically 70-85% accuracy.
